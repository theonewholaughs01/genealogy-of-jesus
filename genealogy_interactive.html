How to read this study

This experience follows Matthew 1:1–17 as its primary genealogy and keeps Matthew’s three fourteen-generation sections visible. The date labels are broad educational estimates, not dates supplied by the biblical text.

Sources and interpretation notes

Luke 3:23–38 presents a distinct genealogy, tracing through David’s son Nathan rather than Solomon. Explanations for the two lists differ among Christian traditions and scholars; this page identifies such proposals as interpretations instead of presenting them as settled fact. Read the primary texts at Matthew 1:1–17 and Luke 3:23–38.
